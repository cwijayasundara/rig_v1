Question: given three confirmed journeys, does a live session write a driver that runs green, gets approved, and fails when the app breaks? Source: 2026-10-21-app.jsonl. Scored by running the driver on the good app and on two seeded breaks (one keeps every unit test green).

## Notes on this run (harness readiness 2.1)

- Live `/rig-app` sessions on Sonnet (claude-sonnet-5-5), 3 runs, about $0.12 and 14 turns each, no permission denials. The person's three journeys were given in the prompt, including the expected 900-cent checkout total.
- 3/3 wrote a valid app definition, driver and feature map, ran green on the working app, and were approved by `app approve`. Both seeded breaks were caught in 3/3: the server that stops reading request bodies (every unit test stays green, so only the running app shows it) and a checkout total off by one cent.
- What this does not show: that a model finds the right journeys unprompted (they were supplied), that it copes with an app much larger than a six-file shop, or a UI app; only Sonnet was tried; n=3 is directional.
