# J1 chart sprint 1 (/rig-chart 1 @docs/prd/sprint-1-library-desk.md)
The PRD is docs/prd/sprint-1-library-desk.md. The chart lives in .rig/product/sprints/sprint-1/. Cite file:line.

- J1.1 [block] The generated requirements ledger (requirements.md) lists every acceptance criterion, data rule and NFR of the PRD, none invented and none dropped. The five FRs (FR-1..FR-5) are covered through their ACs: the ledger carries no FR heading lines, and that is fine.
- J1.2 [block] Every intent behaviour cites at least one FR-n or NFR-n id that the PRD defines (an AC id alone does not count, rig's rule), and every FR and NFR of the PRD is cited by at least one behaviour.
- J1.3 [block] There are three intents (storage, books, loans or close equivalents) and the dependencies match the PRD: books depends on storage, loans on storage and books.
- J1.4 [block] Each desk business rule (DR-4 the limit of 3 loans, DR-5 the 14 day period, DR-6 no borrowing with an overdue loan, DR-7 a free copy is required) is quoted with its exact numbers in a behaviour that cites it, and appears in an acceptance criterion or test case.
- J1.5 [block] The test plan (cases) covers every AC: each AC id appears in at least one case.
- J1.6 [block] The open question OQ-1 about overdue was resolved once, the answer is recorded (Decisions or an AC marked answered at chart), and no open question remains in the chart.
- J1.7 [block] No behaviour builds a non-goal (fines, reservations, renewals, reports, a member registry, a command line, editing or removing a book).
- J1.8 [warn] The loan limit and loan period appear as one named rule each, not duplicated across intents.
- J1.9 [warn] Each intent is small enough for one change (a handful of behaviours) and states its failure behaviour.
